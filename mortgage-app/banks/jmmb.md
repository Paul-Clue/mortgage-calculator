## Age and Repayment

> **IMPORTANT:** JMMB uses age **65** (not 70) as the standard cutoff for employed borrowers. This is **DIFFERENT** from all other banks in this guide.


| Detail                                                                 | Value            |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Maximum age at maturity (employed)                                     | **65 years old** |
| Maximum age at maturity (self-employed, good health, non-physical job) | **70 years old** |
| Minimum age                                                            | 18 years old     |


- **Formula:** `max term = lesser of 35 years OR (65 - borrower's current age)`
- Creditor life insurance is required.

## Residency (Residents vs Diaspora)

- Both residents and non-residents are **eligible**.
- Interest rate is **NOT dependent on residency status**.
- **Currency matters:** USD mortgage attracts a lower rate for those earning in USD.
- NHT access for diaspora via External Financing Mortgage Programme (EFMP).
  - Requires earning over J$30,000/week.

### Diaspora Documentation

- Financial statements
- Tax returns
- National ID
- TRN (Tax Registration Number)
- Proof of address
- Birth certificate

---

## Total Debt Service Ratio (TDSR)

- **TDSR Threshold: 45%** of gross monthly income.
- **Includes:** All monthly loan repayments (existing + proposed mortgage + credit card minimums).
- **Formula:** `TDSR = (Total monthly loan repayments / Gross monthly income) × 100`
- If TDSR exceeds 45%: consolidate debts, make larger down payment, choose cheaper property, or extend tenure.

---

## Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratios

- **Maximum LTV:** 95% of purchase price or market value (whichever is **lower**)
- **Minimum equity/deposit:** 5%

## Minimum Down Payments


| Recommendation           | Amount                                                 |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Absolute Minimum         | **5%** (since up to 95% financing is available)        |
| Recommended              | **20-25%** for stronger application and lower payments |
| Closing Costs (separate) | 5-6% of purchase price + GCT                           |

## Special Programs

### No Legal Fees

- JMMB covers legal fees on their mortgages

### 2-Month Payment Holiday

- Available at the start of the mortgage

### Free Pre-Approval

- Within **48 hours**; can be emailed or picked up at any branch

### NHT Joint Financing (EFMP)

- Up to **J$7.5M** from NHT at NHT rates + additional JMMB financing
- Available to diaspora borrowers

### Unsecured Top-Up

- Up to **J$1M** in unsecured loans for additional home-buying expenses

### Other Features

- Refinancing available from other institutions
- Gift certificates with Spaces Ltd. and Appliance Traders Ltd. for furnishing discounts
- Essential worker packages (historical)
- Self-employed accepted: 2 years financial statements + 12 months bank statements
- Joint applications allowed (combines income for higher qualification)
- Investment property eligible at same 95% max LTV
- Creditor life insurance required
