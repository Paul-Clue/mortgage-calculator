## Age and Repayment

- **Maximum age at loan maturity:** 70 years old
- **Minimum age to apply:** 18 years old
- **Formula:** `max term = min(40, 70 - borrower's current age)` in years
- **Example:** A 45-year-old gets a maximum of 25 years (70 - 45 = 25)

### Extended Mortgage (Beyond Age 70)

Repayment beyond age 70 is possible if the borrower meets **all** of the following:

1. Has assignable life insurance policies
2. LTV is 75% or less, OR TDSR is at least 10% below the ceiling
3. Has additional liquidatable assets
4. Income is expected to continue beyond 70 (pension, rental income, self-employment)

## Residency (Residents vs Diaspora)

### Residents (Jamaica-based)

- Standard JMD rates apply (9.85% for home purchase)
- Up to 90% LTV
- Up to 40 years repayment

### Diaspora (Non-Residents)

- Access foreign currency mortgages (USD/CAD/GBP) at lower rates (5.75%–6.75%)
- Lower LTV: 70% for FX loans
- Shorter max term: 15 years for FX mortgages
- Must be of **Jamaican nationality**
- Can apply via phone, JN Bank LIVE online, or ONE JN Passport mobile app
- Documentation must be notarized

---

## Total Debt Service Ratio (TDSR)

- JN Bank does **NOT** publicly disclose their exact TDSR ceiling.
- Industry standard in Jamaica: approximately **40%** of gross monthly income.
- **Best estimate: ~40%** (with possible flexibility up to ~45%).

## Special Programs

### Mortgage Plus

- Up to **95% financing** + up to **$5M unsecured** for deposit/closing costs

### Graduated Mortgage

- Payments start low and increase over 5 years
- Ideal for young professionals with income growth potential

### Balloon Payment Option

- Lump sum payment due at end of the loan term

### Payment Holiday

- Deferred payments available

### Joint Financing with NHT

- JN Bank offers joint financing with the National Housing Trust (NHT)

### Blending Options

- Borrowers can mix fixed and adjustable rate portions
