## Age and Repayment

- **Maximum age at loan maturity:** 70 years old (industry standard)
- **Formula:** `max term = 70 - borrower's current age`
- **Example:** A 50-year-old gets max 20 years; a 60-year-old gets max 10 years

### Exceptions (Beyond Age 70)

Repayment beyond age 70 may be possible if:

1. Borrower has assignable life insurance policies
2. LTV is 75% or less, or TDSR is at least 10% below the prescribed ceiling
3. Borrower has additional liquidatable resources
4. Income is reasonably expected to continue beyond 70 (pension, rental, self-employment)

> **Note:** Scotiabank Jamaica does not explicitly publish its age-at-maturity policy. The age 70 rule is the Jamaica banking industry standard.

## Total Debt Service Ratio (TDSR)

- Scotiabank recommends: max **40%** of gross monthly income for housing + all debts.
- Under certain conditions, borrowers may qualify for more than 40%.
- Broader Jamaica market benchmark (JMMB): **45%** threshold.
- **Best estimate: 40%** (with flexibility up to 45%).

### How It's Calculated

`TDSR = (All monthly loan payments + proposed mortgage payment) / Gross monthly salary`

**Example:** If gross monthly income is J$300,000 and total existing debt payments are J$26,000, the monthly amount available for housing = J$300,000 × 0.40 - J$26,000 = **J$94,000**.

## Special Programs

### Saver Mortgage

- For customers who have been saving with Scotiabank

### Non-Saver Mortgage

- For new customers or those without a savings history

### Instant Qualifier

- Pre-qualified mortgage option

### Switch Mortgage

- Competitive rates for customers transferring from another lender
- No switch costs, up to 2 months of no payments

### NHT Joint Financing (EFMP)

- NHT maximum loan: **J$23,000,000**
- Combined NHT + Scotiabank can enable up to **100% financing**

### BCIC Insurance

- Mortgage protection insurance offered
- During promotions: free BCIC Insurance up to 2% of loan amount (applied to 1st year annual premium)

### Digital Access

- Applications via Scotia Caribbean Mobile App or Scotia Online
- 46 branches, 120+ ATMs, 200+ merchant kiosks across Jamaica
