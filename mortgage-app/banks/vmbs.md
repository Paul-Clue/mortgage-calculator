## Age and Repayment

- **Maximum age at loan maturity:** 70 years old
- **Formula:** `max term = 70 - borrower's current age`
- **Example:** A 45-year-old gets a maximum of 25 years (70 - 45 = 25)
- The 100% Mortgage explicitly states terms are "based on age of applicant"

## Residency (Residents vs Diaspora)

### Residents (Jamaica-based)

- All mortgage products available
- JMD and Foreign Currency options

### Diaspora (Non-Residents)

- Access most products **EXCEPT** the 100% Mortgage for Specialised Groups
- **VM Diaspora Hub** facilitates overseas Jamaicans:
  - No requirement to return to Jamaica to close on a mortgage
  - Digital document submission or overseas offices (Florida, New York, UK)
  - Same competitive rates and financing terms (up to 97% LTV)
  - Property management via PropMan digital platform
- No evidence of different interest rates for diaspora vs local residents

## Total Debt Service Ratio (TDSR)

- VMBS does **NOT** publicly publish their specific TDSR threshold.
- Industry standard: less than **45%** of gross monthly income.
- **Best estimate: ~45%** (may be stricter at 40%).

## Special Programs

### Joint VMBS/NHT Financing

- Single application for combined NHT benefits

### 100% Mortgage for Specialised Groups

- **J$1.4 billion fund** for self-employed / micro-business owners (taxi drivers, hairdressers, plumbers, etc.)
- Up to **J$15M** for residential property; up to **J$3M** for land purchase
- Requires 6+ months VMBS membership with active savings
- First-time homeowners only; must permanently reside in Jamaica
- Flexible repayment: weekly, fortnightly, or monthly
- Processing fee: 0.50% plus GCT, capped at J$58,250

### iSave for Home Program

- 12-month contractual savings account (min J$5,000/month, US/CAD $1,000/month, or GBP 75/month)
- Qualifies for preferred mortgage rates and up to 97% financing
- 15% rebate on VM Property Services valuation fees
- No mortgage processing fees

### Disaster Recovery Loan

- Up to **J$15M** for home repairs after natural disasters
- 10.85% interest rate
- 2-month payment moratorium
- Funds disbursed within 5-7 business days

### Other Notable Features

- **No minimum income requirement**
- **No repayment penalties** (explicitly stated for construction loans; likely for other products)
- **In-house property registration** (faster and less expensive)
