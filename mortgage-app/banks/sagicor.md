# Sagicor Bank

> Research conducted: March 14, 2026
> Rates and terms are subject to change. Contact Sagicor Bank directly for the most current information.

---

## Interest Rates

| Detail | Rate |
|---|---|
| Promotional Rate (Score Big promo) | As low as **6.75%** per annum |
| Standard Mortgage Range | **8% to 12%** per annum |
| APR Range (includes fees) | **9% to 14%** |
| Rate Types | Both **fixed and variable** rates available |

- Variable rates adjust based on Bank of Jamaica policy rate changes.
- The 6.75% promotional rate may be time-limited.

## Age and Repayment

- **Maximum age at loan maturity:** 70 years old
- **Maximum loan term:** 35 years
- **Formula:** `max term = lesser of 35 years OR (70 - borrower's current age)`

### Age Examples

| Borrower Age | Max Available Term |
|---|---|

| 35 | 35 years (capped at max) |
| 40 | 30 years (70 - 40) |
| 45 | 25 years |
| 50 | 20 years |

## Residency (Residents vs Diaspora)

- Sagicor does **not** publicly detail specific diaspora mortgage terms.
- Joint financing with NHT is available (diaspora can access NHT if contributing for 2+ years).
- Non-resident down payment may be higher (10-20% vs standard).
- **Contact Sagicor directly for specific diaspora terms.**

## Total Debt Service Ratio (TDSR)

- Sagicor does **NOT** publicly state their TDSR threshold.
- Industry standard: **45%** of gross monthly income.
- **Best estimate: ~45%.**

## Special Programs

### General Features

- **Loan amounts:** J$5M to J$100M+
- **No early repayment penalties**
- **Processing time:** Conditional approval in **45 days or less**
- **Digital application** via eBank portal or mobile app

### Unsecured Line of Credit

- Up to **J$2M** alongside the mortgage for home improvements (furniture, solar panels, landscaping, etc.)

### Special Home Insurance

- Discounted home insurance rates for mortgage borrowers

### NHT Joint Financing

- Available for combined mortgage facilities

### Construction Mortgage

- Separate construction mortgage product available

### Fees

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Origination fees | 1-2% of loan amount |
| Appraisal fee | J$5,000 |
| Legal fee | J$10,000 |
| Late payment fees | 5% monthly on arrears |
