## Maximum Loan Terms

| Loan Type | Max Term |
|---|---|
| Home Mortgage | Up to **30 years** (360 months) |
| Secured Loan (may apply to land) | Up to **15 years** (180 months) |
| Unsecured Loan | Up to **5 years** (60 months) |

- Land purchases would likely fall under the mortgage or secured loan product.

## Age and Repayment

- **Maximum age at loan maturity:** 70 years old (industry standard)
- **Formula:** `max term = 70 - borrower's current age`
- **Example:** A 45-year-old gets a maximum of 25 years

### Seniors (70+)

May qualify with conditions:

1. Assignable life insurance policies
2. LTV of 75% or less
3. Additional liquid assets
4. Continuing income (pension, rental, self-employment)

> **Note:** NCB does not explicitly publish their age-at-maturity policy. The age 70 standard is the Jamaica banking industry norm.

## Residency (Residents vs Diaspora)

### Residents

- Standard terms apply
- Must be employed 6+ months with salary paid to NCB account for 6+ months

### Diaspora (Non-Residents)

- NCB does **NOT** prominently market diaspora mortgage products
- NCB closed its UK Representative Office (December 31, 2024)
- Diaspora must use digital channels
- Down payment likely **10-20%** for non-residents
- Standard loan eligibility states "Jamaican resident" for branch loans

---

## Total Debt Service Ratio (TDSR)

- NCB does **NOT** publicly disclose their TDSR threshold.
- Industry standard: less than **45%** of gross monthly income.
- **Best estimate: ~45%.**

## Special Programs

### NHT Joint Financing (EFMP)

- Combine NHT loan (**0-4% interest**) with NCB commercial mortgage

### NCB Dream Home Portal

- Available at [myhome.jncb.com](https://myhome.jncb.com)
- Includes mortgage calculator and application portal

### Eligibility Requirements

- Currently employed for at least 6 months
- Salary paid to NCB account for at least 6 months
- No overdue NCB loans or credit card payments in past 6 months

### What NCB Does NOT Offer

- Dedicated land loan product
- Reverse mortgages
- Publicly listed diaspora mortgage packages
