- **Minimum age:** 18 years old
- **Formula:** `max term = 70 - borrower's current age`

## Residency (Residents vs Diaspora)

### Residents

- Standard terms apply

### Diaspora (Non-Residents)

- FirstCaribbean does **NOT** advertise a dedicated diaspora mortgage product.
- Non-residents **CAN** obtain mortgages (in USD or JMD).

#### Non-Resident Requirements

- Tax returns for last 2 years
- Credit report (minimum score **660**)
- Job letter and pay slips (last 3 months)
- Valid government ID and social security card
- Banker's reference or bank statement (3 months)
- Jamaican TRN required

#### Industry Norms for Non-Residents

- Higher down payment (closer to **20%** vs 5-10% for residents)
- Approval may take **~90 days**
- Closing costs can be **10-15%** of purchase price
- Local attorney and real estate agent recommended

## Total Debt Service Ratio (TDSR)

- FirstCaribbean does **NOT** publicly state their TDSR threshold.
- Their guidance: "Monthly mortgage payment should not exceed a third of net salary" (~33% of net for mortgage alone).
- Industry standard: **40-45%** for total debt.
- **Best estimate: 40-45%** of gross income.

## Special Programs

### Homeowner's Savings Plan (HOSP)

- Set a mortgage goal (1-5 years), save toward 5-20% of intended mortgage amount
- Benefits: preferential savings interest rates, no monthly fees
- Discounted mortgage rates (**0.15-0.30% discount**) upon reaching the savings goal
- Up to **US$5,000** in additional loans for closing costs

### NHT Partnership (EFMP)

- One of 10 NHT mortgage partners in Jamaica
- NHT contributors access full NHT loan benefits at competitive rates
- Particularly beneficial for lower-income earners (earning between minimum wage and J$30,000.99 weekly)

### Additional Products

- Construction loans
- Home equity loans
- Refinancing and mortgage switching options

### Required Insurance

- **Life insurance** for the full value of the loan
- **Home insurance** (for home loans)
- **Mortgage indemnity insurance** (if deposit < 20%; not applicable for land purchases)
